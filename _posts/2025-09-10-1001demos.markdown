1001-Demos is an effective data augmentation framework for robot manipulation, that scales single fisheye, eye-in-hand, obstacle-free, human demonstrations into action-view consistent demos with visually realistic observation & physically feasible action trajectories. By leveraging a novel fisheye-adapted Gaussian Splatting formulation and trajectory optimization, it generates deployable smooth, collision-free demos from novel viewpoints with added obstacles — making robot manipulation policies more robust to unseen configurations and learn to plan around obstacles.
